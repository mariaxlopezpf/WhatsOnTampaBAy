Book-anytime studios & spots to follow

  • AR Workshop St. Pete, 2805 Dr. MLK Jr. St N: DIY wood signs, chunky knit, candle pouring and seasonal crafts; classes posted on their site.
  • The Candle Pour, EDGE District: walk in and blend your own candle from 100+ fragrance oils.
  • Morean Center for Clay: one-time Throw & Fire sessions on Saturdays by appointment ($75), plus the weekly workshops above.
  • Glass of Life, Historic Uptown: beginner stained-glass make-and-take workshops.
  • Imagine Museum, 1901 Central Ave: monthly candle and stained-glass workshops ($45–$60), plus live music Fridays. Free for local heroes plus a guest all November.
  • Craft Club Cinema at Green Light Cinema: a monthly Sunday screening where you bring your knitting or craft project. November's film wasn't posted yet.
  • Ferg's Sports Bar: November is on the calendar above. Coming in December: Lou Gramm (Dec 4), St. Pete Gift Grab (Dec 8) and Twisted Sister (Dec 11), plus the usual trivia, open jam and football nights. Full Ferg's calendar.
  • Moxy St. Pete has hosted Latin dance nights on Thursdays, worth checking their Eventbrite for November dates.

About this calendar

  • Compiled November 2, 2026 from organizer sites, Meetup, Eventbrite, the St. Pete Chamber calendar, Visit St. Pete/Clearwater, St. Pete Catalyst, I Love the 'Burg, St. Pete Pier and race listings.
  • Instagram and Facebook pages can't be read automatically, so groups that post schedules only there (Sunrise Crew, Pinellas Run Club, Off Balanced) link to their page; check their latest post before heading out.
  • On SweatPals, most St. Pete schedules only load inside the app. Worth following there: St. Pete SUP (free sunset paddles off North Shore), Local: A Lagree Fitness Studio (free community walks from Perry's Porch) and Club Track (listed above).
  • I Love the 'Burg only showed listings through Nov 7 when this was compiled; later weeks appear on their site each Thursday.
  • RSVP links go to the registration, ticket or signup page. Where a group doesn't take RSVPs, the event says "Just show up." Get Fit St. Pete classes need a free Get Fit membership, which the link covers.
  • Events marked Verify run on a fixed pattern (first Tuesday, second Saturday) and their November dates were worked out from it.
